1. Institution Overview
Dawood University of Engineering and Technology, commonly known as DUET, is a public sector university
located in Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. It was originally established in 1962 and was elevated to full university
status in 2013. The university serves as a major engineering and technology institution in Sindh. Students can
apply online through the official admission portal at admissions.duet.edu.pk.
2. Campuses
DUET operates across three campuses:
Jinnah Campus: The main campus where all B.E. Engineering programs are held.
Gulberg Town Campus: Houses the Faculty of Information and Computing Science, where all B.S.
Computing and Applied Sciences programs are conducted.
Iqbal Campus: Located in Gulshan-e-Iqbal and is dedicated to the Bachelor of Architecture program.
Students applying for B.Arch. must be aware that they will be studying at a completely separate campus
from engineering students.
3. Programs Offered
3.1 B.E. Engineering Programs
All B.E. programs are four-year undergraduate degrees offered at the Jinnah Campus. These programs are PEC
accredited. To be eligible, students must have a minimum of 60% aggregate in HSC Pre-Engineering or hold a
DAE in a relevant technology.
B.E. Chemical Engineering: Focuses on process engineering and industrial chemistry.
B.E. Electronic Engineering: Covers circuitry, embedded systems, and signal processing.
B.E. Industrial Engineering and Management: Integrates engineering principles with business
management to optimize complex processes.
B.E. Metallurgy and Materials Engineering: Focuses on the development and processing of materials.
B.E. Energy and Environment Engineering: Addresses sustainable energy solutions and
environmental conservation.
B.E. Petroleum and Gas Engineering: Tailored for the upstream and downstream oil and gas sector.
B.E. Computer System Engineering: Bridges the gap between hardware and software engineering.
IMPORTANT: B.E. Telecommunication Engineering has been permanently discontinued and is NOT
available from Fall-2025 onwards. The chatbot must never suggest or recommend this program to any
applicant under any circumstances.
3.2 B.S. Computing and Applied Sciences Programs
All B.S. programs are four-year undergraduate degrees offered at the Gulberg Town Campus.

4. Admission Process
4.1 How Merit is Calculated — CPN Formula
DUET uses a Composite Percentage Number, known as CPN, to rank all applicants and determine merit.
The Formula: Entry Test score carries 60% weightage, HSC result carries 30% weightage, and SSC or
Matriculation result carries 10% weightage. So the CPN equals 0.6 multiplied by the Entry Test
percentage, plus 0.3 multiplied by the HSC percentage, plus 0.1 multiplied by the SSC percentage.
Minimum Requirement: The minimum CPN required to be eligible for admission is 50. Any applicant
who scores below a CPN of 50 is completely disqualified and cannot be admitted regardless of how
many seats are available.
4.2 Entry Test
The entry test consists of 100 multiple choice questions. There is no negative marking, meaning students are
not penalized for wrong answers.
Pre-Engineering Group: 25 questions from English, 10 from Analytical Reasoning, 15 from General
Knowledge, 20 from Physics, 10 from Chemistry, and 20 from Mathematics.
Computer Science Group: 25 questions from English, 10 from Analytical Reasoning, 15 from General
Knowledge, 20 from Physics, 10 from Mathematics, and 20 from Computer Science. Note that

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & HISTORY
Dow University of Health Sciences (DUHS) is a public sector medical and health sciences university
located in Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. The university was formally established in December 2003 by the
Government of Sindh, incorporating the historic Dow Medical College (DMC), which was originally
founded in 1945. The university is named in honor of Dr. Ruth K.M. Pfau, a celebrated physician and
humanitarian.
DUHS is ranked among the top three medical universities in Pakistan according to the Higher Education
Commission (HEC). It maintains a global alumni network of over 17,000 healthcare professionals. The
university's motto is "To Heal | To Educate | To Discover."
The university is fully recognized and accredited by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of
Pakistan. All medical and dental programs strictly adhere to the regulations set by the Pakistan Medical
Commission (PMC), formerly known as the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PM&DC;).
DUHS is affiliated with Civil Hospital Karachi and operates its own teaching hospitals, including Dow
University Hospital at Ojha Campus. The university is known for its experienced faculty, advanced
research infrastructure, and strong clinical training environment.
SECTION 2: CAMPUS LOCATIONS
DUHS operates primarily from two main campuses in Karachi, Sindh.
The first is the historic Dow Medical College (DMC) Campus, located on Baba-e-Urdu Road in central
Karachi. This campus houses the original Dow Medical College and the Institute of Business and Health
Management (IBHM), which runs the EMBA program on weekends.
The second is the Ojha Campus, which is the larger and more modern hub of the university, located in
the Ojha area of Karachi. It houses Dow University Hospital, the School of Postgraduate Studies, Dow
College of Pharmacy (DCOP), Dow Institute of Medical Technology (DIMT), Dow Institute of Cardiology
(DIC), Dow College of Biotechnology (DCOB), the School of Public Health, and the student hostels. The
IBHM at Ojha Campus runs BBA and MBA programs during morning and evening sessions.
SECTION 3: UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS
DUHS offers a wide range of undergraduate degree programs across medicine, dentistry, allied health
sciences, and business.

The flagship medical program is the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS), a five-year
program divided into ten semesters. It follows an integrated teaching approach combining theoretical
knowledge with hands-on clinical experience. MBBS is offered at Dow Medical College (DMC) and Dow
International Medical College (DIMC).
The Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) is offered at Dow Dental College (DDC), Dow International
Dental College (DIDC), and the Dr. Ishrat ul Ebad Khan Institute of Oral Health Sciences (DIKIOHS).
The Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D) is a five-year professional degree offered at Dow College of
Pharmacy (DCOP) in both morning and self-finance evening programs.
The Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) is a five-year program focused on rehabilitation and
physiotherapy with practical clinical exposure at DUHS facilities.
BS Nursing is a four-year undergraduate program offered for both male and female students.
BS Biotechnology is a four-year program offered at Dow College of Biotechnology (DCOB).
BS Medical Technology is a four-year program at Dow Institute of Medical Technology (DIMT). A
separate specialization, BS Medical Technology in Nuclear Medicine, is also offered at DIMT.
BS Optometry and Visual Sciences is a four-year program at DIMT.
BS Cardiovascular Technology is a four-year program at Dow Institute of Cardiology (DIC).
BS Forensic Sciences, BS Radiologic Technology, BS Dental Care Professionals, BS Public Health, BS
Psychology, and BS Nutrition and Food Sciences are all four-year undergraduate programs offered at
their respective schools and institutes.
The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is offered as both a two-year and four-year program at
IBHM, Ojha Campus. A BS in Accounting and Finance (BSAF) is also offered as a four-year program at
IBHM.
SECTION 4: POSTGRADUATE AND PhD PROGRAMS
DUHS provides comprehensive postgraduate programs through its School of Postgraduate Studies and
various constituent institutes.
The Master of Business Administration (MBA) is a two-year program offered at IBHM in morning and
evening sessions at Ojha Campus. An Executive MBA (EMBA) is a two-year weekend program at DMC
Campus.
Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) is a postgraduate dental specialization. Master of Health Professions
Education (MHPE) is a master's program in health education. Master of Public Health (MPH) is offered
through the School of Public Health.
MS in Management Sciences, MS in Advanced Practice Therapy (MSAPT), MS in Biomedical
Engineering (MSBE), and Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) are research-based master's programs.

MPhil and PhD programs are offered in various disciplines. The PhD in Pharmacology (Pharmaceutical
Sciences) runs for three to eight years. PhD applicants must hold a minimum CGPA of 3.0 and pass the
university's qualifying test or an HEC-recognized GAT Subject examination.

DUHS is structured across multiple constituent colleges and institutes.
Dow Medical College (DMC), established in 1945 on Baba-e-Urdu Road, is the flagship medical college

Dow International Medical College (DIMC), established in 2007, admits 150 MBBS students annually,

Dow Dental College (DDC), Dow International Dental College (DIDC), and Dr. Ishrat ul Ebad Khan

Dow College of Pharmacy (DCOP) offers Pharm-D. Dow College of Biotechnology (DCOB) offers BS

Dow Institute of Medical Technology (DIMT) offers BS Medical Technology, BS Optometry and Visual

Dow Institute of Cardiology (DIC) offers BS Cardiovascular Technology.

Institute of Business and Health Management (IBHM) offers BBA, BSAF, MBA, and EMBA programs.

SECTION 6: ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Eligibility requirements at DUHS vary by program level and type.
For MBBS and BDS at public sector seats (DMC, DDC), applicants must have passed HSSC
Pre-Medical or an equivalent such as A-Levels (IBCC-certified) with a minimum of 60% marks, as per
PMC rules. A valid MDCAT score meeting PMC requirements is mandatory. Domicile and Permanent
Residence Certificate (PRC) of Sindh province are mandatory for public sector seats.

For Dow International Medical College (DIMC), the minimum FSc marks requirement is 65% in HSSC
Pre-Medical or equivalent, and a minimum 60% score on the National MDCAT is required.
For allied health programs (BS Nursing, BS Medical Technology, BS Cardiovascular Technology, BS
Radiologic Technology, BS Optometry, BS Forensic Sciences, BS Dental Care Professionals, BS Public
Health, BS Psychology, BS Nutrition, DPT, and Pharm-D), applicants must have a minimum of 60%
marks in HSSC Pre-Medical or equivalent. Candidates must score at least 50% in both FSc and the
DUHS entry test to be eligible for merit listing.
For the MBA, applicants need 16 years of education with a minimum of 50% marks. For general
Master's programs, a minimum CGPA of 2.5 or 60% marks in the relevant undergraduate degree is
required. PhD applicants require a minimum CGPA of 3.0.
SECTION 7: ENTRY TESTS
An entry test is mandatory for all degree programs at DUHS. No exemptions are granted.
For MBBS and BDS, the National MDCAT (Medical and Dental College Admission Test) is required. In
Sindh, IBA Sukkur administers the MDCAT on behalf of the PMC. It is held once annually, typically in
September or October. The MDCAT covers Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical
Reasoning. It consists of 200 MCQs to be completed in 150 minutes. There is no negative marking.
The minimum MDCAT score for MBBS eligibility is 65% (130 out of 200 marks). The minimum MDCAT
score for BDS eligibility is 55% (110 out of 200 marks).
For allied health programs including BS Nursing, BS Medical Technology, BS Cardiovascular
Technology, BS Radiologic Technology, BS Optometry, BS Forensic Sciences, DPT, and Pharm-D,
DUHS administers its own internal DUHS Entry Test at Ojha Campus. The minimum passing mark for
most BS programs is 50%. For Pharm-D specifically, the minimum passing mark in the DUHS entry test
is 25%.
For business programs (BBA, MBA) at IBHM, the DUHS Entry Test evaluates quantitative and verbal
skills. The minimum passing score for the MBA entry test is 50%.
The merit formula for BS programs is 50% FSc marks and 50% DUHS entry test score. For MBBS and
BDS, merit is based on SSC marks at 10%, HSSC marks at 40%, and MDCAT score at 50%. The final
aggregate is rounded to four decimal places.
SECTION 8: APPLICATION PROCEDURE
The entire admission process at DUHS is conducted online through the official admissions portal at
admissions.duhs.edu.pk or admission-duhs.effii.app. Physical application forms are not accepted.
Applicants must create an account on the portal, fill in personal and academic details, select the desired
program and seat category, upload required documents, and pay the application fee online.

For MBBS and BDS, applicants must also register separately with the PMC/UHS portal for the national
MDCAT process.
For Pharm-D, DPT, and BS programs, applications are submitted directly on the DUHS portal. Entry test
details are communicated through the DUHS website after application.
SECTION 9: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
A complete application must include the filled online application form, HSSC or A-Level transcripts, SSC
or O-Level transcripts, CNIC or B-Form copy, passport-sized photographs, and MDCAT or DUHS entry
test result slip.
A-Level applicants must provide an IBCC equivalence certificate for both O-Level and A-Level
qualifications.
For public sector medical and dental seats, an original Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC) and
domicile certificate from the tagged districts of Sindh province are mandatory.
For postgraduate programs, additional documents include the undergraduate degree certificate and
official transcripts.
SECTION 10: ADMISSION DEADLINES
Application deadlines vary by program and intake session. Deadlines are hard limits — applications

Admission Policy (2025-2026) 
(From Official FAST National University Eligibility Criteria Page — 
nu.edu.pk/Admissions/EligibilityCriteria, updated August 6, 2025)

Merit Formula 
 
FAST National University uses a clearly defined merit formula that differs by program 
category. There is no interview for undergraduate admissions at any campus. 
 
For Bachelor of Science Engineering programs including Civil Engineering, Electrical 
Engineering, and Computer Engineering, the merit score is calculated as 33% weightage on 
the admission test score, 50% weightage on the Higher Secondary Certificate or equivalent 
result, and 17% weightage on the Secondary School Certificate or equivalent result. The 
minimum eligibility requirement is 60% marks in both the Secondary School Certificate 
and the Higher Secondary Certificate. The required subjects at Higher Secondary 
Certificate level are Pre-Engineering covering Chemistry, Mathematics, and Physics, or the 
Intermediate Certificate of Science covering Computer Science, Mathematics, and 
Physics. 
 
For Bachelor of Science Computing programs including Computer Science, Artificial 
Intelligence, Cyber Security, Data Science, and Software Engineering, the merit score is 
calculated as 50% weightage on the admission test score, 40% weightage on the Higher 
Secondary Certificate or equivalent result, and 10% weightage on the Secondary School 
Certificate or equivalent result. The minimum eligibility requirement is 60% marks in the 
Secondary School Certificate and 50% marks in the Higher Secondary Certificate. 
Mathematics must have been studied at the Higher Secondary Certificate level. Pre-
Medical students who have passed additional Mathematics, or who are awaiting results for 
additional Mathematics, are also eligible to apply for Computing programs. 
 
For Bachelor of Business Administration and Business-related Bachelor of Science 
programs including Business Analytics, Accounting and Finance, and Financial Technology, 
the merit formula is identical to the Computing programs: 50% admission test, 40% Higher 
Secondary Certificate, and 10% Secondary School Certificate. The minimum requirement 
is 60% in Secondary School Certificate and 50% in Higher Secondary Certificate. There is 
no specific subject restriction at the Higher Secondary Certificate level for business 
programs.

For Master of Science programs in Computing and Engineering disciplines, the selection is 
based on 50% weightage on the admission test score and 50% weightage on the 
undergraduate academic record. Eligibility requires a Bachelor degree from a recognized 
university after 16 years of education with a minimum of 60% marks or a Cumulative Grade 
Point Average of at least 2.00 on a scale of 4.00. 
 
For Master of Science in Mathematics and Applied Linguistics, the minimum eligibility 
threshold is reduced to 55% marks or a Cumulative Grade Point Average of at least 2.00 on 
a 4.00 scale. 
 
For Master of Science in Software Project Management, in addition to academic 
qualifications, applicants must possess a minimum of two years of professional 
experience related to Software Development Lifecycle activities as a software engineer or 
equivalent role in a software development firm. 
 
For PhD programs, eligibility requires an 18-year education pathway with a Master of 
Science, Master of Philosophy, or equivalent degree with a minimum of 60% marks or a 
Cumulative Grade Point Average of at least 3.00 on a 4.00 scale. PhD merit is determined 
as 60% weightage on the admission test score and 40% weightage on a panel interview 
assessing research potential. The minimum passing mark in the admission test is 60%. 
Students with a Cumulative Grade Point Average between 2.90 and 3.00, or between 58% 
and 60% in the annual system, may be provisionally admitted subject to successful 
completion of a Zero Semester, during which they must study nine additional credit hours 
of Level 7 courses and achieve a minimum Grade Point Average of 3.00. For the PhD in 
Management Sciences, selection criteria are based on performance in the Graduate 
Management Admission Test or an equivalent test, an interview and assessment by the 
Doctoral Admissions Committee, and submission of a Research Proposal.

(From Official FAST National University Admissions Page — nu.edu.pk/Admissions and

The FAST National University Admission Test, known as FNET, is a Computer-Based Test

English language skills. A minimum passing score, which varies by campus and program, is

Applicants for undergraduate programs must select only one of three test options: the FAST 
National University Admission Test, the Scholastic Assessment Test, or the National Testing 
Service National Aptitude Test under the relevant category. Note that National Testing

Weightage of Higher Secondary Certificate marks is calculated based on whichever is

Chairmen equivalence certificate for Advanced Level or Ordinary Level qualifications.

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & HISTORY
Jinnah Sindh Medical University (JSMU) is a public sector medical university located in Karachi, Sindh,
Pakistan. It is the largest medical university in the province of Sindh, with a total student enrollment
exceeding 6,000 across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.
Source: JSMU Official Website (jsmu.edu.pk) — About Page; TopUniversities Profile of JSMU
The university traces its origins to Sindh Medical College (SMC), which was established on April 7,
1973, after approval of a committee constituted by the Government of Sindh. The college initially
operated within the premises of Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC). Approximately 200
students were admitted in the first batch of MBBS in 1973.
Source: Wikipedia — Jinnah Sindh Medical University; JSMU Official About Page
In 2003, Sindh Medical College became a constituent of Dow University of Health Sciences (DUHS).
Later in 2010, it merged with Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre. The college formally achieved
university status on June 1, 2012, through a legislative ordinance signed by the Governor of Sindh,
Ishrat-ul-Ebad Khan, in the presence of President Asif Ali Zardari at Bilawal House.
Source: Wikipedia — Jinnah Sindh Medical University; JSMU Official About Page
JSMU is fully recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan and the University
Grants Commission (UGC). All medical and dental programs are regulated by the Pakistan Medical
Commission (PMC). JSMU currently has 11 constituent institutes and approximately 35 affiliated
colleges and programmes.
Source: Wikipedia — Jinnah Sindh Medical University; HEC University Listing; JSMU Official About Page
The university's motto is 'Knowledge, Service, and Character.' Its mission is to be a socially
accountable, nationally benchmarked institution aspiring to improve health through education,
leadership, scholarship, and community service.
Source: JSMU Official About Page (jsmu.edu.pk/about.html)
JSMU is a pioneer of the modular method of teaching in public sector medical universities in Pakistan. It
was the first public sector medical university to administer computer-based semester and final

Wi-Fi to students and faculty.

Institutional Identity and History 
 
The University of Karachi, commonly known as Karachi University, is the premier public 
research university of Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. It is recognized globally and is positioned 
within the top 8.9% of universities worldwide according to the Center for World University 
Rankings for 2025, while maintaining a ranking of 13th nationally within Pakistan. The QS 
World University Rankings for 2026 places the institution within the 1001 to 1200 bracket 
globally. 
 
Operating as both an educational and an affiliating examining body, the university 
influences the academic trajectories of tens of thousands of students annually. The 
internal academic architecture is immense, comprising over 50 distinct academic 
departments across multiple faculties including Arts and Social Sciences, Science, Islamic 
Studies, Engineering, Law, Pharmacy, Management, and Education. 
 
Campus Infrastructure 
 
The University of Karachi operates from its main campus located on University Road, 
Karachi. The campus houses all primary faculties and departments under a single 
institutional body. The university also functions as an affiliating university, overseeing the 
academic standards of numerous colleges across the city and province. 
 
Academic Programs 
 
The university offers a vast range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs spanning 
sciences, humanities, professional disciplines, and applied fields. Programs are offered in

both Morning and Evening shifts. The Morning shift serves as the standard regular program, 
while the Evening shift functions as a supplementary, revenue-generating parallel offering. 
 
Bachelor of Science Programs — Morning 
 
The university offers Bachelor of Science programs across four broad pricing tiers based on 
discipline, laboratory requirements, and market demand. These include programs in 
Arabic, Bengali, Islamic History, Persian, Philosophy, Sindhi, Urdu, Islamic Learning, Usool-
ud-Din, Quran and Sunnah, Chinese, History, Library and Information Science, Pakistan 
Studies, Political Science, Social Work, Women’s Studies, Education, Special Education, 
Digital Media Marketing, Economics, Economics and Finance, English, International 
Relations, Mass Communication, Psychology, Sociology, Teacher Education, Applied 
Physics, Botany, Genetics, Geography, Geology, Health and Physical Education, 
Mathematics, Marine Science, Physics, Statistics, Zoology, Applied Chemistry, 
Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Chemistry, Food Science, Institute of Space Science and 
Technology, Microbiology, Physiology, Poultry Science, Public Health, Financial 
Mathematics, Computational Mathematics, Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Criminology, 
Public Administration, Actuarial Science and Risk Management, Environmental Studies, 
Agriculture and Agribusiness Management, Chemical Engineering, Sports Business 
Management, Visual Studies, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, School of Law, 
Petroleum Technology, Karachi University Business School offering Bachelor of Business 
Administration, and Doctor of Pharmacy. 
 
Bachelor of Science Programs — Evening 
 
The Evening program offers the same academic disciplines as the Morning program for 
most departments, with substantially elevated fee structures. Evening admissions are 
governed by a lower merit threshold, effectively making the financial capacity of the 
student the primary barrier to entry rather than academic performance. 
 
Entrance Test Based Specialized Programs

For professional and specialized disciplines, the university requires candidates to clear a

Admission Policy (2025) 
 
Merit Formula and Admission Categories 
 
The University of Karachi uses a geography-based admission sorting system formally 
known as the Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan policy, commonly referred to as the KSP policy. This 
policy stratifies applicants into three sequential priority tiers based on where the applicant 
completed their Secondary and Higher Secondary Certificate education. 
 
Category Karachi: This is the primary and highest-priority tier. It is reserved for applicants 
who completed their Secondary Certificate and Higher Secondary Certificate from 
institutions physically situated within Karachi. 
 
Category Sindh: This is the secondary priority tier, applying to candidates whose 
educational certificates were obtained from recognized institutions within Sindh province, 
excluding Karachi. 
 
Category Pakistan: This is the tertiary priority tier, encompassing all remaining applicants 
whose qualifications were obtained from institutions outside Sindh but within Pakistan.

Admissions are filled sequentially: Karachi category applicants are considered first, 
followed by Sindh category applicants if seats remain, and finally Pakistan category 
applicants if further seats are still available. This system has faced significant legislative 
scrutiny, with provincial oversight bodies directing the university to transition toward a 
unified, province-wide open merit system. However, the KSP stratification remained deeply 
embedded in the 2025 admission cycle. 
 
Eligibility Criteria 
 
The university establishes an absolute statutory minimum of 45% in the Higher Secondary 
Certificate as the floor for eligibility across all open merit programs. Programs under the 
entrance test based system require candidates to also achieve a minimum score of 50 out 
of 100 on the university entrance test in order to proceed to academic merit ranking. 
Certain programs operating under reduced demand conditions have lowered this test 
minimum to 45, 40, 35, 30, or even 25 in order to fill institutional capacity. A small number 
of programs are fully exempted from the test requirement. 
 
For the School of Law program, candidates must additionally provide a valid Higher 
Education Commission Law Admission Test score of a minimum of 50 marks, 
compounding the entry barrier beyond the standard test. 
 
Application Process 
 
Students must apply through the official University of Karachi admissions portal. The 
application and processing procedures follow standard public university protocols, with 
documentation requirements including Higher Secondary Certificate and Secondary 
Certificate transcripts, national identity documents, and domicile certificates where 
applicable. 
 
Entry Test Details

The University of Karachi administers its own internal entrance test for specialized and 
professional programs. The standard passing mark for the entrance test is 50 out of 100. 
Candidates who score below the required minimum on the entry test are automatically 
disqualified regardless of their intermediate academic percentage. The entry test acts as a 
first filter before the academic merit percentage is applied for final ranking. 
 
Fee Structure (Session 2025) 
 
The University of Karachi follows a deliberate tiered pricing strategy for its Morning 
programs, based on faculty grouping, laboratory infrastructure requirements, and the 
perceived market value of the degree. All morning program fees include an embedded 
admission fee of Rs. 1,000. The university has mandated a compounding annual fee 
escalation of 10% per year for the batch of 2025 onward, meaning the total cost of a four-
year degree increases significantly beyond the initial intake fee.

The lowest fee bracket, ranging from Rs. 26,000 to Rs. 31,000 per semester, covers

Arabic, Bengali, Islamic History, Persian, Philosophy, Sindhi, Urdu, the Faculty of Islamic

The standard sciences and social sciences bracket, ranging from Rs. 34,000 to Rs. 43,000

Sociology, Teacher Education, Applied Physics, Botany, Genetics, Geography, Geology, 
Health and Physical Education, Mathematics, Marine Science, Physics, Statistics, Zoology, 
Applied Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Chemistry, Food Science, Institute of

The specialized and emerging disciplines bracket, ranging from Rs. 52,000 to Rs. 82,500 per

and Dietetics, Criminology, Public Administration, Actuarial Science and Risk 
Management, Environmental Studies, Agriculture and Agribusiness Management,

The elite corporate and healthcare premium bracket, ranging from Rs. 86,000 to Rs.

Petroleum Technology, Karachi University Business School Bachelor of Business

Evening Program Fee Structure 
 
The Evening program charges a mandatory non-refundable upfront administrative overhead 
at the start of each academic year. This consists of an admission fee of Rs. 4,500, an 
enrolment fee of Rs. 1,500, a development fee of Rs. 2,500, and a refundable caution 
money deposit of Rs. 1,500, bringing the total initial amount to Rs. 10,000. This Rs. 10,000 
is added to the base tuition fee at the time of admission. 
 
Evening tuition fees are substantially higher than Morning fees across all departments. 
Mass Communication students face a premium of over 117% compared to their Morning 
counterparts. English and International Relations students face a markup of approximately 
66%. Standard sciences such as Microbiology and Biotechnology carry an evening 
premium of approximately 72%. Computer Science carries an evening premium of 
approximately 21.5%, while Karachi University Business School Bachelor of Business 
Administration carries the smallest relative premium at approximately 8%, as pushing 
those fees further would exceed the financial capacity of the target demographic. 
 
Credential Upgrading and Diploma Programs

The university also offers Bachelor of Science Third Year lateral entry programs through the 
Evening shift, designed for mid-career professionals and students holding legacy two-year 
degrees who need to upgrade to a four-year Bachelor of Science equivalent. Fees for Third 
Year entry broadly mirror the Evening First Year structure. 
 
Additionally, the university offers postgraduate diploma and certificate courses targeting

Petroleum Technology are standardized at Rs. 17,000 tuition plus Rs. 10,000 admission, 
totaling Rs. 27,000.

The University of Karachi remains the largest and most comprehensive public research

Pakistan. Its 2025 admission cycle reflects an institution navigating a profound transitional 
period, simultaneously functioning as a guardian of subsidized public education and a 
revenue-generating commercial enterprise. 
 
The concentration of high closing percentages in Computer Science, Pharmacy, and 
Business Administration programs, consistently ranging from 75% to 81%, stands in sharp 
contrast to the total collapse of entry standards to the 45% statutory floor in fundamental 
sciences and humanities. This reflects a broader cultural shift within the applicant pool, 
which is increasingly utilizing the university as a vocational conduit for corporate 
employment rather than foundational academic research. 
 
The Evening program operates functionally as a pay-to-play educational model: by lowering 
open merit requirements to the 45% floor while simultaneously inflating tuition fees by 60% 
to 117% over regular morning rates, the university has created a system where financial 
capital, rather than academic merit, becomes the primary determinant of evening program 
access. 
 
The KSP geographical policy continues to create stark inequities, with students from 
interior Sindh facing admission penalties of 20% to 44% higher academic requirements 
compared to Karachi-domiciled counterparts for the same program and the same 
remaining seats, a disparity that has drawn significant political and legislative criticism. 
With compounding 10% annual fee escalations mandated for the 2025 batch onward, the 
university faces the challenge of replacing geographic exclusion with economic exclusion if 
structural reforms are not implemented.

Karachi Metropolitan University (KMU) is a public sector university located in Block M, North

Source: KMU Official Website — KMDC Page (thekmu.edu.pk/karachi-medical-dental-college); Wikipedia — Karachi

national medical education sector.
Source: KMU Official Website — KMDC Page; MBBS.org.pk KMDC Admission Profile
KMDC initially enrolled 50 MBBS and 25 BDS students per year. Over time, annual intake grew to 250
MBBS and 100 BDS students, reflecting its expanded capacity and recognition.
Source: KMU Official Website — KMDC Page (thekmu.edu.pk/karachi-medical-dental-college)

SECTION 8: ENTRY TEST
An entry test is mandatory for admission to all programs at KMU. KMDC holds the distinction of being
the first government sector medical institution in Pakistan to introduce an entry test for MBBS and BDS
admissions.
Source: KMU Official KMDC Page (thekmu.edu.pk/karachi-medical-dental-college); MBBS.org.pk KMDC Profile
For MBBS and BDS, the National MDCAT (Medical and Dental College Admission Test) is mandatory,
conducted by the Pakistan Medical Commission (PMC) through IBA Sukkur in Sindh. The MDCAT
covers Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning in a 200 MCQ format, 150 minutes,
with no negative marking. The minimum MDCAT score for MBBS eligibility is 65% (130 out of 200) and
for BDS is 55% (110 out of 200) per PMC rules.
Source: PMC MDCAT Regulations; PMDC.org.pk KMU Profile 2025
The overall merit threshold for MBBS and BDS at KMU is highly competitive. A minimum aggregate of
approximately 80% is typically required to secure a seat. Admissions are merit-based and
top-performing candidates may be called for interviews as part of the selection process.
Source: PMDC.org.pk KMU Profile 2025

Institutional Identity and History 
(From Official SSUET Website — ssuet.edu.pk/about-us and Wikipedia — Sir Syed 
University of Engineering and Technology) 
 
Sir Syed University of Engineering and Technology, commonly known as SSUET, is a private 
research university located in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. The university is 
named in honor of the notable 19th-century Muslim reformer, educationist, and philosopher 
Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, whose Aligarh Movement formed the ideological foundation of the 
institution. 
 
History: The university came into being on 8 October 1993, founded by Z.A. Nizami, who 
was also the former Director General of the Karachi Development Authority and President 
of the Aligarh Muslim University Old Boys Association, commonly known as AMUOBA. Its 
Charter formalities were completed by an Act passed by the Sindh Assembly on 17 
September 1995, granting it the status of a degree-awarding institution. The university was 
established in the spirit of the Aligarh Movement — to advance modern scientific and 
technological education among the youth of Pakistan. 
 
Motto: The official motto of the university is the Quranic verse meaning “He (Allah) has 
taught man that which he knew not. ” The university colors are purple, gold, and olive green. 
 
Recognition and Rankings: SSUET is fully recognized by the Higher Education Commission 
of Pakistan. It is ranked among the top universities in Pakistan in the engineering and 
technology category by the Higher Education Commission. The university is also a member 
of the Association of Commonwealth Universities of the United Kingdom and a member of 
the International Association of Universities.

Merit Formula 
 
Admission merit at SSUET is calculated based on a combination of the applicant’s previous 
academic record, specifically the Higher Secondary Certificate result, and the 
performance in the SSUET Entry Test. The final merit list is generated after the entry test 
results are processed and candidates are ranked accordingly.

Eligibility Criteria 
 
For undergraduate programs, students must have passed the Higher Secondary Certificate 
examination or an equivalent qualification with the required minimum percentage for their 
chosen program. Students who are currently registered for Higher Secondary Certificate 
Part Two or equivalent supplementary examinations in 2025 may also apply on a 
provisional basis, provided they submit a current Admit Card or Statement of Entry. 
Admission remains provisional until final results are submitted and eligibility is confirmed 
within the specified deadline. 
 
Pre-Medical students are eligible to apply for Computing and Technology programs. 
However, they must successfully complete a Mathematics Foundation Course during their 
first year at the university as a mandatory deficiency requirement. 
 
Students from equivalent boards, including those with Cambridge Ordinary Level and 
Advanced Level qualifications, must submit an Inter Board Committee of Chairmen 
equivalence certificate in order to proceed with their admission. 
 
For postgraduate Master of Science programs, candidates must hold an appropriate 
undergraduate degree and must have passed the SSUET Graduate Assessment Test or a 
National Testing Service General Aptitude Test with a minimum of 50% marks prior to 
admission. A mandatory interview conducted by the Departmental Postgraduate 
Committee must also be cleared. 
 
For PhD programs, candidates must hold a recognized Master of Science, Master of 
Philosophy, or equivalent degree with a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average of 3.0 
out of 4.0 or First Division in the annual system. The SSUET Graduate Assessment Test or 
Higher Education Commission Approved General Aptitude Test must be passed with a 
minimum of 60% marks. A statement of purpose and a departmental interview are also 
required. 
 
Admission Categories

SSUET offers admissions under two categories. The Open Merit category is for students 
who qualify based on their academic record and entry test performance. The Self-Finance 
category is available for students who do not meet the open merit cutoff but still wish to 
secure a seat at the university at a higher fee. 
 
Application Process 
 
Students must apply online through the SSUET admissions portal available on the official 
university website. The application fee voucher must be paid at MCB Bank and uploaded to 
the portal. Payments made through the PAYPRO online option are verified automatically. 
Payments made close to the deadline take two to three working days to verify. Inter-bank 
Fund Transfer payments are not permitted. On-campus payment is available on the day of 
the entry test for students who missed the deadline. 
 
Entry Test Details 
(From Official SSUET Undergraduate Admissions Page — 
ssuet.edu.pk/admissions/undergraduate-admissions/admission-test-details and 
ilmkidunya.com) 
 
The SSUET Entry Test is a mandatory component of the undergraduate admission process. 
The test is conducted on campus and students must carry their admit card, original 
National Identity Card or Bay Form, a paid test fee voucher, stationery, and a calculator. 
Mobile phones and smart watches are strictly prohibited during the examination. 
 
Students have the flexibility to choose the type of entry test based on their subject 
background from intermediate or equivalent qualifications. The test is designed to assess 
aptitude across the relevant subjects of study. 
 
Entry Test Exemption: SSUET allows exemption from its own entry test for students who 
have already appeared in and passed any of the following tests in 2025: the Undergraduate

Scholastic Assessment Test, the NED University Entry Test, the Mehran University of 
Engineering and Technology Entry Test, the National Testing Service test, the Educational 
Testing and Evaluation Agency test, the Medical and Dental College Admission Test, or any 
Public Sector University Entry Test. Students can claim exemption by uploading a valid 
scorecard with their application or by visiting the Admissions Office before the deadline. 
Exempted students may still optionally attempt the SSUET test to improve their score, in 
which case the highest score will be used in merit calculation.

SSUET charges fees on a per-credit-hour basis combined with a set of fixed semester

Tuition is charged at a per-credit-hour rate. For the Computer Science program as an

year exceeds Rs. 200,000. Fee refunds for new admissions follow the rules of regulatory

Scholarships and Financial Assistance 
(From SSUET Official Scholarship Notification and Criteria — ssuet.edu.pk/students and 
Studocu — SSUET Scholarship Criteria 2022) 
 
SSUET offers internal scholarships and financial assistance to eligible students every 
semester. The scholarship grant covers 48% to 54% of the tuition fee and is awarded based 
on academic merit and financial need. The university scholarship program is designed to 
prevent financial difficulties from being a barrier to academic continuity. 
 
Scholarship categories include merit-based awards for students who achieve the highest 
academic performance in their semester, brother and sister category awards for families 
with two or more students enrolled simultaneously at SSUET, and financial assistance for 
genuinely deserving students facing economic hardship. 
 
Key conditions governing the scholarship program are as follows. Only students who are 
regular and enrolled in a full course load in the previous semester are eligible to apply. 
Students who have cleared any subject through supplementary or resit examinations are 
not eligible for scholarship for that semester. Students in their 8th semester, or their 10th 
semester in the case of Architecture students, are not eligible to apply. Sons and daughters 
of SSUET staff members are not eligible. Students who are already receiving a scholarship 
from any external source are not eligible for SSUET scholarships simultaneously. A freeship 
award means 100% of the tuition fee is waived. The decision of the Scholarship Committee 
is final and cannot be challenged. The Gold Medal awarded at convocation requires a 
minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average of 3.5.

External scholarships available to SSUET students include Higher Education Commission 
Need-Based Scholarships and international grants such as the Heydar Aliyev International 
Education Grant Program for Non-Aligned Movement member states.

SECTION 7: ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Eligibility criteria at SMBBMC vary by program. All programs require applicants to meet minimum
academic qualifications and pass a mandatory entry test.
Source: Ilmkidunya.com SMBBMC Admission Guide; MedicalStudy.pk SMBBMU Admission Guide
For MBBS: Applicants must have completed FSc Pre-Medical or an equivalent qualification with a
minimum of 60% marks from a recognized Board of Education. A-Level applicants must provide an
IBCC equivalence certificate. The age requirement is 17 to 25 years at the time of application.
Applicants must have a valid MDCAT score as per PMC regulations. Sindh domicile and Permanent
Residence Certificate (PRC) are required for all public sector seats.
Source: Ilmkidunya.com SMBBMC Admission Details; MedicalStudy.pk SMBBMU Admission Guide; SMBBMU
Larkana Admissions Page
For BDS: The eligibility criteria mirror those of MBBS — minimum 60% in FSc Pre-Medical, age 17 to 25
years, valid MDCAT score, and Sindh domicile/PRC for public sector seats.
Source: Ilmkidunya.com SMBBMC Admission Details; DUHS Admissions Portal — BDS Merit Lists 2025-26
For BS Nursing (BSN) Generic Program: Applicants must have passed Matric (SSC) in Science group
with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects from any Sindh Secondary Board, with a
minimum of 55% marks. OR applicants must have passed FSc Pre-Medical with Physics, Chemistry,
and Biology with a minimum of 50% marks from any Sindh or Federal Board. Only candidates with
Sindh domicile are eligible. For the Lyari Quota seats, the candidate must hold Domicile/PRC
specifically from Lyari Town.
Source: SMBBMC Official BSN Admission Advertisement 2025 (Result.pk); Result.pk — SMBBMC BSN Admission
Details 2025
For Overseas Pakistani applicants: Must provide proof that their father, mother, or parents are working
outside Pakistan. They may also apply under the UEAP/UDP/Overseas Self-Finance category.
Source: SMBBMU Larkana Admissions Page — Overseas Eligibility Criteria
For Foreign National applicants: Nomination by the Higher Education Commission (HEC), Islamabad is
required. Applicants must also submit SSC/O-Level and HSC/A-Level equivalence certificates from
IBCC.
Source: SMBBMU Larkana Admissions Page — Foreign National Eligibility Criteria
For FCPS-II Postgraduate Programs: Applicants must hold an MBBS degree from a university
recognized and listed in Schedule II of the Pakistan Medical Commission (PMC). A valid PMC
registration as a medical practitioner is mandatory. Completion of one year of resident house job in a
PMC-recognized institution is required. For FCPS-II specifically, the candidate must have passed
FCPS-I in the relevant specialty prior to applying.
Source: SMBBMC Official Postgraduate Admission Advertisement, May 29, 2025 (Ref:
F-SMBBMCL/(P.Gs)/2024-25/3605-06)
For MCPS Postgraduate Programs: Applicants must hold MBBS, have valid PMC registration, and have
completed one year of house job in relevant departments from a PMC-recognized institution.

Source: SMBBMC Official Postgraduate Admission Advertisement, May 2025
SECTION 8: ENTRY TESTS
An entry test is mandatory for all undergraduate programs at SMBBMC. Admission is not possible
without passing the relevant entry test.
Source: SMBBMU Larkana Admissions Page; MedicalStudy.pk SMBBMU Admission Guide
For MBBS and BDS: The National MDCAT (Medical and Dental College Admission Test) is mandatory.
In Sindh Province, the MDCAT is administered by IBA Sukkur on behalf of the Pakistan Medical
Commission (PMC). The test covers Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning in a
200 MCQ format over 150 minutes, with no negative marking. The minimum passing score for MBBS is
65% (130 out of 200) and for BDS is 55% (110 out of 200) as per PMC regulations.
Source: PMC MDCAT Regulations; MedicalStudy.pk SMBBMU Admission Guide
For BS Nursing (BSN): The entry test is administered by the National Testing Service (NTS).
Candidates must register at www.nts.org.pk and pay a separate NTS test fee of Rs. 800 through
nominated bank branches or online. NTS will issue admit cards by post or for download on the NTS
website. Candidates are required to achieve a minimum passing score to be eligible for merit
consideration.
Source: SMBBMC Official BSN Admission Advertisement 2025; Result.pk — SMBBMC BSN Admission 2025
Appearance in the NTS or MDCAT entry test does not guarantee admission. Final admission decisions
are based on the merit list prepared after original document verification.
Source: SMBBMC Official BSN Admission Advertisement 2025
